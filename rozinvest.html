Rozinvest — Type & Visual Identity

Building the colour, type, and iconography system for a Gen Z mutual fund platform in India. Every decision rooted in market psychology.

Client Rozinvest
Domain Fintech · Mutual Funds
Scope Color, Type, Iconography
Role Visual Designer
01 — Overview

A visual language for first-time investors

Rozinvest is expert-guided mutual fund investing for Gen Z. The platform tells you what to do with your money — no confusing charts, no jargon. It needed a brand identity that says "your money is safe here" and "this was made for you" in the same breath.

The task: define the colour palette, select typefaces for marketing and in-app use, explore app icon directions, and deliver brand mockups across digital and outdoor touchpoints.

02 — Colour System

Rooted in market psychology, not trends

In Indian fintech, green isn't a creative choice — it's a cultural signal. Green means growth, profitability, "markets are up." Blue signals institutional trust. The palette leverages existing associations, not fights them.

Why no purple in the core palette? Purple has become strongly associated with the crypto market in India — a space carrying declining trust and volatility stigma globally. For a mutual fund platform targeting cautious first-time investors, anchoring on green (growth, profitability) and blue (institutional trust) was the strategically defensible choice. Purple was retained only as an accent colour for campaign-specific, attention-grabbing use.

03 — Typography

Two faces, two jobs

A brand that lives between Instagram reels and portfolio value tables needs two typefaces that never compete. One grabs attention. One delivers numbers without friction.

Guton brings a distinctive, modern voice that elevates brand personality across campaigns and touchpoints. Its expressive yet balanced forms make it highly effective for headlines, social content, and marketing creatives. Strong visual presence with versatility across digital and print.

SF Pro delivers exceptional clarity across sizes, making it highly legible for dense financial data and mobile-first interfaces. Its neutral yet refined character reinforces trust and professionalism — critical for an app handling people's money.

04 — Icon Exploration

Four directions, one winner

The app icon needed to work at 16px on a browser tab and 512px on the App Store. Four concepts were explored — all building on the "R" letterform with visual metaphors for growth.

Wordmark "RI" — clean letterform, simple recognition

Mountain form — growth, strength, power of small efforts

R + checkmark — blending trust with the brand initial

R + growth arrow — upward momentum in the letterform

05 — In Context

From screen to street

The identity was stress-tested across real-world touchpoints — at home screen scale on a device, and at environmental scale in a transit station citylight.

06 — Conclusion

A system built for trust at first sight

Rozinvest's visual identity bridges institutional credibility and Gen Z approachability. Every decision — from green as the primary cultural signal to SF Pro for data legibility — was anchored in how Indian first-time investors already perceive finance.
